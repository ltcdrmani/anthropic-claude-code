Fern Canyon Permit & Entry Rules

Book this before you go — it’s mandatory in summer, and there’s no signal on-site

⚠️ Mandatory Vehicle Day-Use Reservation (May 15 – Sep 15)

A vehicle day-use reservation is required to reach Fern Canyon / Gold Bluffs Beach in summer. Book it in advance — and download or screenshot the confirmation at home, because there is zero cellular service on Davison Road and the beach.

How to book:

  • Portal: redwoodparksconservancy.org/fern-canyon
  • In advance: Book online immediately ($0 reservation fee) — choose a Saturday morning (9 AM–1 PM) or midday slot.
  • Day-before release: Additional permits drop Friday at 9:00 AM PT if your date is sold out.

Kiosk fee: $12 cash/check collected at the Gold Bluffs Beach gate — waived with an America the Beautiful or CA State Parks pass.

Critical tip: Screenshot the reservation confirmation before you leave the Airbnb — you won’t have signal to pull it up at the gate.

Weather & Packing Essentials

Coastal microclimates, wet trails, and gear for the little ones

🌡️ Temperatures

Daytime highs 62–68°F; morning/coastal lows 50–54°F. Pleasant, but never hot — plan for cool starts.

🌫️ Microclimates

Morning coastal fog and canopy drip give way to afternoon sun. Dress in easily removable layers for everyone.

👟 Footwear

Strapped water sandals or water shoes plus spare dry socks for Fern Canyon’s stream crossings. Standard walking shoes for Sunday’s groves.

👶 Baby Gear

A front/backpack carrier for TinTin on all unpaved and boardwalk trails — strollers struggle on roots, stairs, and dirt roads.

🥤 Zero-Oil Vegan Meals

Keep prepared meals in a heavy-duty cooler with block ice or cold packs. Easy reheating at each Airbnb / overnight stop.

📶 No Signal Zones

Zero cell service on Davison Road and Gold Bluffs Beach. Download offline maps and your Fern Canyon permit before you arrive.
