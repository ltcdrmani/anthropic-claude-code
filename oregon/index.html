Day 1

Portland to the Central Oregon Coast

Portland → Coast highlights → Overnight in Lebanon • ~300 mi

🌊
Weather & Gear: Breezy coast — High 62°F / Low 45°F with coastal winds and ocean spray. Pack windbreakers, hooded rain shells, and extra dry socks for the kids.
⚡
Tesla charging: Leave Portland full. Top up early at ⚡ Lincoln City (8×150kW) — it’s the only fast charger on the coast leg; there’s none from Newport south through Florence & the Dunes. Recharge back on I-5 around ⚡ Halsey before reaching Lebanon.
8:00 – 10:15 AM

Drive: Portland → Devils Punchbowl

~110 miles via I-5 South & US-20 West to the coast.

~110 mi • I-5 S / US-20 W

3. Thor’s Well & Spouting Horn

~0.4-mi walk. Watch waves drain into the natural basalt sinkhole from the paved viewpoints. Keep 5-year-old Advaith close by hand — slippery rocks and surging water.

Day 2

Day Trip to Crater Lake National Park

Lebanon → Crater Lake → Overnight in Lebanon • ~340 mi round trip

🏔️
Weather & Gear: High-elevation cold & sunny — High 43°F / Low 27°F. Dress in warm thermal layers, fleece, winter coats, beanies, and gloves. Bundle up before exiting the vehicle.
⚡
Tesla charging — plan this leg: ~320 mi round trip through cold mountains, so start at 100% and charge mid-trip. Nearest fast charger to the park is ⚡ Chemult (8×325kW) on US-97. In the park there’s only a slow 16kW destination charger (Mazama Village) — a top-up, not a real charge. Precondition the battery before the climb.
8:00 – 11:15 AM

Drive: Lebanon → Crater Lake (North Entrance)

~160 miles via I-5 South, OR-58 East & OR-138 West.

~160 mi • I-5 S / OR-58 E / OR-138 W

Day 3

Silver Falls State Park → Home to Seattle

Lebanon → Silver Falls → Seattle • ~265 mi

🌌️
Weather & Gear: Damp, shaded forest — High 64°F / Low 43°F. Trails near the waterfalls stay wet; wear sturdy walking shoes with reliable traction.
⚡
Tesla charging: Easy I-5 day. After Silver Falls, charge at ⚡ Woodburn (12×250kW), then one quick stop into Seattle around ⚡ Centralia or ⚡ Tacoma.

🗽️ Feature Hike: The Winter Falls Loop (~5.0 miles)

Trail: Winter Falls Loop, part of the Trail of Ten Falls network.

Highlights: 7 distinct waterfalls, including the 177-ft South Falls, Lower South Falls, and Winter Falls.

Kid highlight: The trail passes directly behind the curtain of water at South Falls and Lower South Falls — expect mist and slick rock, so hold the little ones’ hands.

8:00 – 8:50 AM

Drive: Lebanon → Silver Falls State Park

~38 miles north via OR-214 N. Parking pass: $10–$12.

~38 mi • OR-214 N Parking pass $10–$12
9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

5-Mile Waterfall Hike (Winter Falls Loop)

Walk behind roaring waterfalls, through lush canyon moss, and along scenic footbridges — seven falls on one loop.

~5 mi loop • 7 waterfalls
12:00 – 1:15 PM

Lunch Break

Adults: packed lunch at the picnic grove. Kids: warm grilled cheese, hot cocoa, or burgers at the South Falls Day-Use Café or nearby in Silverton.

Picnic grove • South Falls Café / Silverton
1:15 – 5:00 PM

~225 miles north via I-5 N (approx. 3.5–4 hours of driving). Arrive Seattle around 5:00 PM — trip complete!

~225 mi • I-5 North Home for ~5:00 PM

Day 3 Dining

  • Adults: Packed lunch at the picnic grove before the drive home.

Weather Forecast & Gear Guide

Three very different climates in three days — pack in layers

🌊 Day 1 — Central Oregon Coast

High 62°F / Low 45°F. Coastal winds and ocean spray. Pack windbreakers, hooded rain shells, and extra dry socks for the kids.

🏔️ Day 2 — Crater Lake NP

High 43°F / Low 27°F (cold / sunny). High-elevation cold — warm thermal layers, fleece, winter coats, beanies, and gloves. Bundle up before stepping out of the car.

🌌️ Day 3 — Silver Falls SP

High 64°F / Low 43°F. Damp, shaded forest trails near waterfalls. Wear sturdy walking shoes with reliable traction — expect mist behind the falls.
